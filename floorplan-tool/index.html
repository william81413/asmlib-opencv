Geometry is read straight out of the vector layer of the architect's Obergeschoss.pdf (03.08.2026), walls classified by fill colour into Bestand / Neu / Abbruch, and checked against the sheet's own dimension chain: 24+3,56⁵+17⁵+3,98+24+2,31⁵+24 = 10,75⁵ — all seven lines match.

Areas are anchored to the NGF printed on the sheet. Moving a wall applies the geometric change to that number. Switch to Raw geometry to see the uncalibrated values: they run 0,55–0,91 m² over the sheet in Schlafen, Kind, Wohnen and Bad — about 3 m² in total, still to be confirmed with the architect.

No ceiling heights are modelled. If the rooms sit under a roof slope, tall furniture may pass the plan check and still not fit.
